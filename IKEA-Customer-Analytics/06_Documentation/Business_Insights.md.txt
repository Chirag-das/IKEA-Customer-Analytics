IKEA Customer Analytics — Business Insights
1. Business objective
The objective is to identify high-value customers and customers at risk of churn using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.

The analysis supports three business functions:
- Marketing: Target customers with relevant campaigns.
- CRM and Retention: Re-engage inactive and declining customers.
- Business Leadership: Understand customer value and prioritize retention spending.
The project uses historical Online Retail II transaction data from December 2009 to December 2011. The BRD defines churn as no purchase activity in the trailing 90 days.
2. Customer segmentation insights
The analysis classifies customers into six segments:
Customer segment	Business interpretation
Champions	Most valuable customers with strong recent, frequent, and high-value purchases
Loyal	Customers with consistent purchase behaviour
At Risk	Previously valuable customers whose recent purchasing activity has declined
New	Recently acquired customers who have not yet established repeat behaviour
Needs Attention	Customers with some recent activity but relatively low purchase frequency
Lost	Customers with no meaningful activity for an extended period

Key observations
1. Lost customers represent the largest segment, at approximately 25.9%. This indicates a substantial group of customers with extended inactivity.
2. Loyal customers account for 24.1% of the customer base, representing an important group to retain through consistent engagement.
3. Champions account for 21.9% of customers. They should receive priority in loyalty and relationship-building initiatives.
4. At Risk customers represent 14% of customers. They should be prioritized for re-engagement before they become Lost customers.
5. New customers account for 7.5%. Encouraging a second purchase can help convert them into Loyal customers.
4. Recommended business actions
Segment	Recommended action
Champions	Exclusive offers, loyalty rewards, early access to new collections
Loyal	Repeat-purchase incentives and personalized recommendations
At Risk	Win-back campaigns, personalized discounts, targeted email reminders
New	Welcome offers and follow-up campaigns to encourage a second purchase
Needs Attention	Product discovery campaigns and low-value purchase incentives
Lost	Targeted reactivation campaigns; avoid spending heavily where engagement remains low

5. Key business recommendations
A. Focus on customer retention

Retaining existing valuable customers should be a priority. Champions and Loyal customers should receive consistent engagement rather than relying only on acquisition campaigns.

B. Reduce customer inactivity

The At Risk and Lost segments should be monitored using the agreed 90-day inactivity definition. Marketing teams can use targeted reactivation campaigns to encourage another purchase.

C. Improve new customer conversion

New customers should receive onboarding communication, relevant product information, and second-purchase incentives.

D. Use customer segmentation for marketing

Instead of sending the same campaign to everyone, IKEA can create different marketing strategies based on customer value and purchasing behaviour.

E. Monitor customer performance regularly

The business should track segment sizes, revenue contribution, customer recency, purchase frequency, and churn indicators over time.
6. Important limitations
- The analysis uses historical transaction data from 2009–2011, not current customer behaviour.
- Churn is defined using a fixed 90-day inactivity threshold.
- Wholesale and individual retail customers are not separated unless the source data supports this distinction.
- The analysis identifies customer segments but does not use a predictive machine-learning churn model.
- Segment counts alone do not establish revenue contribution. Monetary analysis is required to identify the financial value of each segment.
7. Conclusion
The RFM analysis provides a structured approach to understanding customer purchasing behaviour and identifying different levels of customer value.

The segmentation highlights three important priorities: retain Champions and Loyal customers, re-engage At Risk customers, and improve repeat purchasing among New customers. The Lost segment also provides an opportunity to evaluate targeted reactivation strategies.

By using customer-level insights, IKEA can make more informed decisions about marketing priorities, customer retention, and CRM activities.
