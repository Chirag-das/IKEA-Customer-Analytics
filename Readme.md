# IKEA Customer Analytics | Customer Insights & RFM Segmentation

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on customer behaviour, RFM segmentation, customer value, and churn analysis using Excel, SQL Server, Python, and Power BI.

---

## Project Overview

The IKEA Gifting Customer Analytics project aims to understand customer purchasing behaviour, identify valuable customer segments, and highlight customers who may be at risk of churning.

The project is based on the Online Retail II transaction dataset and follows a business-driven analytical approach. It covers data exploration, cleaning, transformation, customer-level RFM analysis, churn identification, and interactive dashboard development.

The analysis is designed to help business stakeholders understand customer value, monitor retention patterns, and make more informed marketing and customer relationship management decisions.

## Business Problem

The business needs a structured understanding of its customer base to answer important questions:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which customers have stopped purchasing and may be at risk of churning?
- How does customer value and retention change over time?
- How does customer performance vary across countries?
- What actions should be considered for different customer segments?

Without customer segmentation and retention analysis, marketing and CRM activities may not effectively prioritize customers based on their value and engagement.

## Project Objectives

1. Clean and prepare transaction-level retail data.
2. Combine and validate data from multiple source files.
3. Calculate Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics.
4. Assign RFM scores and classify customers into meaningful segments.
5. Identify inactive customers using a fixed 90-day churn threshold.
6. Analyse customer value, revenue contribution, and geographic performance.
7. Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate business insights.
8. Present actionable recommendations for customer retention and engagement.

## Project Workflow

The project follows a structured end-to-end analytics workflow.

### 1. Excel – Data Exploration

- Inspected the source data and its structure.
- Reviewed column names and data types.
- Understood the transaction-level information.

### 2. SQL Server – Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Imported the source datasets into SQL Server.
- Combined the two yearly datasets.
- Checked data types and conversion issues.
- Validated missing customer identifiers and invalid values.
- Investigated duplicate records.
- Prepared a cleaned transaction dataset for further analysis.

### 3. Python – RFM Analysis & Customer Segmentation

Used Python to perform customer-level analysis.

The three RFM metrics are:

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Recency (R) | Number of days since the customer's most recent purchase |
| Frequency (F) | Number of distinct purchase transactions |
| Monetary (M) | Total revenue generated by the customer |

RFM scoring is used to classify customers according to their purchasing behaviour and value.

## Customer Segmentation

Customers are classified into six segments based on their RFM scores.

| Segment | Business Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Champions | Highly engaged customers with strong purchasing behaviour and value |
| Loyal | Customers with consistent purchase activity |
| At Risk | Previously valuable customers showing signs of reduced engagement |
| New | Recently acquired customers who have not yet established repeat purchasing behaviour |
| Needs Attention | Customers who require further engagement to encourage repeat purchases |
| Lost | Customers with extended periods of inactivity |

These segments help distinguish customers according to their historical purchasing behaviour and support targeted retention strategies.

## Churn Analysis

A fixed **90-day inactivity threshold** is used to identify potentially churned customers.

**Definition:**

A customer is flagged as churned when no purchase activity is recorded during the trailing 90 days.

This rule provides a simple and interpretable way to identify inactive customers.

Note: This is a rule-based churn flag, not a predictive machine learning model.

## Business Recommendations

The following are potential business actions based on the RFM framework. Final actions should be prioritized using the actual findings from the completed analysis.

| Segment | Suggested Action |
|---|---|
| Champions | Focus on loyalty programmes, exclusive offers, and long-term engagement |
| Loyal | Encourage repeat purchases through personalized campaigns |
| At Risk | Prioritize re-engagement campaigns and monitor purchase activity |
| New | Improve onboarding and encourage a second purchase |
| Needs Attention | Use targeted promotions to increase purchase frequency |
| Lost | Evaluate win-back campaigns based on customer value and campaign cost |

## Project Scope & Limitations

### In Scope

- Historical transaction data analysis.
- SQL-based data preparation.
- RFM customer segmentation.
- Rule-based churn identification.
- Geographic and time-based analysis.
- Interactive Power BI reporting.

### Out of Scope

- Predictive churn modelling.
- Real-time customer scoring.
- Automated marketing campaigns.
- Individual product-level recommendations.

### Limitations

- The analysis is based on historical transaction data.
- Churn is defined using a fixed 90-day threshold.
- Wholesale and individual retail customers are not separated unless the source data supports that distinction.
- External factors such as promotional calendars are not included in the analysis.
